# Writing modes

## Authoring and collaborative drafting

Use the brief, source material, audience, and established voice. If structure is open, propose a compact outline and proceed within the requested authority. Do not force a questionnaire, dozens of brainstorm options, or a confirmation after each section. Ask where a decision affects the content materially. Save files only where the task authorizes it.

When feedback arrives, make precise revisions. Preserve an author's explicit wording choices unless they cause a concrete problem. A fresh-reader check can expose assumptions if one is available. Without one, self-check reader questions and label the review as your own. Do not claim a simulated reader is an independent person or a real customer.

## Editing

Distinguish correctness, clarity, tone, persuasion, and preference. Give a concrete reason for material edits. Preserve quotations and factual relationships. Do not improve specificity by supplying an unsupported number. Follow the requested scope before performing a full-document style pass.

`create-content` owns reusable editorial guidance in this collection. Apply the user's and project's active style rules without requiring a separate writing skill. Do not duplicate a separate global ban list inside every specialist or present an author's deliberate style as an objective error.

## Marketing

Identify the audience, product truth, desired action, and evidence. Draft the argument from these facts. Keep benefits supportable. A request for more persuasive copy does not authorize inventing a guarantee, certification, customer logo, testimonial, scarcity deadline, or expected result.

When a claim is unsupported, name the gap and offer honest wording. Do not confuse a model's impression that copy converts with measured conversion evidence. A panel of model personas is an exploratory lens, not an independent expert review or participant study.

## Communication

Match the audience, period, confidentiality, requested format, and intended channel. Distinguish completed work from planned, staged, blocked, and shipped work. Do not invent business impact. Keep uncertainty and ownership clear without assigning blame from incomplete information.

A status update can use Progress/Plans/Problems when appropriate, but user headings win. An incident communication should distinguish observed facts, impact, mitigation, and unresolved cause. A customer message should not promise fixes or timelines beyond the evidence and authority supplied.

Return the draft for the requested endpoint. Sending, scheduling, publishing, contacting stakeholders, and changing a shared source document require the corresponding capability and authority. Availability of a connector is not permission to use it.
