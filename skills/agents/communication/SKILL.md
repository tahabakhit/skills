name: communication
description: "Use when drafting or editing substantial prose, reviewing a document, or teaching a concept. Not needed for routine status replies or code review."
---

Choose the reader, purpose and intended result before shaping the prose. Preserve
an existing author's stance, terminology and uncertainty. Clearer writing must
not imply stronger evidence. Follow the user's style, not a universal house voice.

Fix a missing point, unsupported claim or wrong structure before replacing words.
Prefer concrete actors, mechanisms and examples over generic benefits. Do not
manufacture facts, anecdotes, humour or authority to make prose sound human.
Preserve exact code, commands, paths and quoted material.

Load only the branch needed:

- Drafting, rewriting or diagnosing prose: `references/writing.md`.
- READMEs and user-facing technical documents: also `references/technical-writing.md`.
- A lesson, worked explanation or consequential procedure: `references/explain-and-teach.md`.
- Reviewing a document, proposal or plan: `references/non-code-review.md`.
- Presenting substantial research findings: `references/exploration.md`.

Return the requested artifact or material judgment. Do not impose the artifact's
structure on later chat replies. Feedback can suggest future improvements, but
this skill does not authorize editing global instructions or skills automatically.
