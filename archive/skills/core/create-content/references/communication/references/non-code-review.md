# Editorial self-check

During an authorized draft/revision, check whether the reader can find the point,
understand the mechanism and act on the intended result. An unsupported claim,
wrong structure or missing context matters more than polishing words.

Preserve author intent. An exact passage and representative reader scenario can
explain a material ambiguity without a taste-based rewrite. Keep observation,
interpretation and uncertainty distinct; do not invent a concern to fill a checklist.

This is an author's quality lens, not the document-review workflow. Requested
factual/numerical/inferential findings belong to an available document-review skill.
Do not convert review-only work into editing, or call this self-check independent
acceptance. The owning create-content entrypoint supplies output and stopping rules.
