# Audience and explanation judgment

The create-content entrypoint owns drafting, revision, evidence and handback. These
are optional editorial lenses, not another writing/review workflow. Choose only the
needed branch; links are relative to this guide:

- Author voice and genre: [writing](references/writing.md).
- User-facing README/product information: [technical writing](references/technical-writing.md).
- Lessons, worked explanations or consequential procedures: [explain and teach](references/explain-and-teach.md).
- Author self-check of a draft or proposal: [editorial self-check](references/non-code-review.md).
- Presenting research findings: [exploration presentation](references/exploration.md).

For a requested factual, numerical or inferential review, use an available
document-review owner instead of this editorial branch. If unavailable, assess the
supplied claims within evidence limits without pretending to supply specialist
review. An author self-check is not an independent review and does not authorize a
rewrite, publication or durable instruction change.
